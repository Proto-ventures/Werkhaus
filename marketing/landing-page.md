# Landing page copy

The page is a finished shift report with the prompt box above it. Describing the
output would ask the reader to take our word for it, and that is the one thing
this product cannot ask for.

Rules this copy is held to, because breaking them is what makes a page read as
machine-written:

- No dash used to join two clauses. Use a full stop.
- No "not X, but Y". No "isn't just X, it's Y".
- No lists of three where two or four would do.
- Vary sentence length. Some sentences should be short.
- Concrete numbers over adjectives. £29, six boxes, three sources.
- No section that restates a section above it.

## The report

Everything below is one example report, rendered in full at the size it actually
arrives. It is labelled as an example. We are pre-launch and it is not a
customer's company.

**Header row:** example report / Northwind Ceramics / shift 1 / 12 min / $6.84

**Typed into the box**

> A monthly subscription box for hand-thrown ceramics. One nice object a month,
> made by a real potter, sent to people who live in small flats and don't want
> more clutter.

**Eight desks.** Each name, their job, the file they left behind, and the mark on
it.

**From market-research.md.** The actual table, not a summary of it. Marked
sourced because the three pages it came from are attached. A document cannot
carry that mark without them.

| Box | Price / mo | What arrives | Names the maker? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pottery Box | £18 | 2-3 small pieces | No |
| Clay Collective | £29 | 1 piece + a zine | Yes |
| The Pottery Box | £32 | 1-2 pieces, seasonal | Yes |
| Handmade Home | £24 | mixed homeware | No |
| Northern Clay | £45 | 1 large piece, quarterly | Yes |
| Muddy Hands | £22 | beginner's kit | n/a |

*Two more appear in search results but their sites are dead. Excluded rather than
guessed at.*

**One decision, of two.**

> **Price at £29/mo, not £9**
>
> Every box in the table that names its maker sits at £29 or above, and naming
> the maker is the whole pitch.
>
> Instead of:
> £9/mo, which sits below the boxes that send unbranded seconds.
> £45/mo quarterly, which Northern Clay already owns and which kills the habit.
>
> **Vera contested this.** Reasoned from competitor pricing, not from any
> willingness-to-pay evidence. Defensible, not validated.

**Vera, end of shift.** She cannot edit anything. Three objections minimum, each
naming a claim and what would settle it.

> **serious** / positioning.md
> The £29 price is defended entirely with competitor pricing. Not one person in
> the target audience has been asked what they would pay.
> *Settled by:* five conversations with people who match the audience, asking
> what they currently spend on objects like this.

> **serious** / unit-economics.md
> Contribution of £9.85 rests on three cost lines that are guesses, and breakage
> is missing from the model entirely.
> *Settled by:* one quote from a potter and one from a courier.

> **noted** / channels.md
> The speciality-coffee overlap is labelled a hunch, which is honest, but it is
> also the cheapest thing on the list to check and nobody was asked to check it.
> *Settled by:* an afternoon in one coffee forum.

**Where it left the company.** 64%.

> Positioning and price are settled and the page is live. Nobody has said they
> would buy it.

Still missing:
No evidence any real person will pay £29.
The cost model has no supplier quotes behind it.
Zero waitlist signups so far.
